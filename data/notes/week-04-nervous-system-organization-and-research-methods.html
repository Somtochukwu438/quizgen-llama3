Original Online Lecture

Organizing the nervous system

The central nervous system includes brain and spinal cord. The peripheral system includes somatic and autonomic divisions; sympathetic and parasympathetic branches coordinate changing bodily demands.

Check your understanding: Explain this concept in your own words and connect it to one earlier course concept.

Major brain systems

Brainstem structures support vital regulation; thalamic and hypothalamic regions coordinate sensory and homeostatic functions; basal ganglia, limbic networks, cerebellum, and cortex contribute through interconnected circuits.

Check your understanding: Explain this concept in your own words and connect it to one earlier course concept.

Methods

MRI describes structure; fMRI estimates activity through blood-oxygen changes; EEG/ERP offer fine temporal resolution; stimulation and lesion studies can strengthen causal inference but have important constraints.

Avoiding reverse inference

A region becoming active during a task does not prove that the region has one exclusive function or that the participant experienced a specific mental state. Converging methods produce stronger conclusions.

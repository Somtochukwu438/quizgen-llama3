Original Online Lecture

Two-process regulation

Circadian timing and homeostatic sleep pressure interact. The suprachiasmatic nucleus coordinates daily rhythms using light input, while sleep pressure generally rises with time awake.

Check your understanding: Explain this concept in your own words and connect it to one earlier course concept.

Arousal systems

Distributed brainstem, hypothalamic, basal forebrain, and cortical systems regulate alertness. Orexin/hypocretin helps stabilize wakefulness; disruption is associated with narcoleptic symptoms.

Check your understanding: Explain this concept in your own words and connect it to one earlier course concept.

Sleep architecture

NREM sleep progresses through characteristic EEG patterns and deeper slow-wave activity; REM sleep includes cortical activation, vivid dreaming, rapid eye movements, and muscle atonia.

Functions and disorders

Sleep supports learning, emotional regulation, metabolic health, immune function, and safety. Insomnia, apnea, circadian disorders, parasomnias, and narcolepsy involve different mechanisms and treatments.
